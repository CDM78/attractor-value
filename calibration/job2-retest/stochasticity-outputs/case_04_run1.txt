JUSTIFICATION: The risk profile has meaningfully deteriorated between filings. Two entirely new risk factors were added (physical climate change effects and supply chain/inflation), reflecting newly materialized operational threats. Across the retained risk factors, the dominant pattern is a shift from hypothetical language to language acknowledging realized adverse conditions: natural gas costs "have increased," COVID-19 "has adversely affected" operations, inflation is explicitly noted as occurring, and solar panel shortages and government tariffs are cited as active supply chain disruptions. Multiple risk factors added new specificity about emerging threats (ransomware attack vectors, ESG-related financing restrictions, Great Resignation workforce pressures, coal supplier financial hardship). The sole removal -- the SOX internal controls risk factor -- is modest in significance compared to the breadth of escalation elsewhere.
