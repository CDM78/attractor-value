OBSERVATIONS_C:
1. Cumulative trauma claims: Prior year reported "1,481 cumulative trauma lawsuits comprised of 2,355 claims at December 31, 2018"; current year reported "1,605 cumulative trauma lawsuits comprised of 2,456 claims at December 31, 2019." This is an increase of 124 lawsuits and 101 claims, reflecting continued growth in legacy product liability exposure.
2. Insurance receivables: Prior year reported "insurance receivables totaling $71.7 million and notes receivable of $59.6 million"; current year reported "$63.8 million and notes receivables of $56.0 million," representing a decline in available insurance recovery.
3. Reserve language: Prior year described reserves as established for "cumulative trauma product liability claims currently asserted and estimated incurred but not reported ('IBNR')"; current year slightly modified to "estimated amounts for cumulative trauma product liability claims currently asserted but not yet resolved and incurred but not reported ('IBNR')." This adds "not yet resolved" language but is a minor wording change.
4. Reserve adjustment language: Current year modified the factors influencing reserve adjustments, adding: "Each of these factors may increase or decrease significantly within an individual period depending on, among other things, the timing of claims filings or settlements, or litigation outcomes during a particular period that are especially favorable or unfavorable to MSA LLC. We accordingly consider MSA LLC's claims experience over multiple periods and/or whether there are changes in MSA LLC's claims experience and trends that are likely to continue for a significant time into the future in determining whether to make an adjustment to the reserve, rather than evaluating such factors solely in the short term." This expanded language provides more detail on methodology but does not fundamentally change the risk.
5. Acquisition risk: Prior year referenced Globe (2017 acquisition); current year referenced Sierra Monitor Corporation (2019 acquisition) as the most recent acquisition example.
6. Oil/gas market risk: Prior year stated "the volatility in upstream, midstream and downstream markets"; current year simplified to "the volatility in the oil, gas and petrochemical industry." Minor wording change.
7. Competition description: Current year added "technology, cost of ownership, comfort" to the basis of competition (prior year had "functional performance, agency approvals, design and style").
8. International operations: Prior year said "over 40 foreign countries"; current year said "approximately 40 foreign countries." Minor.
9. International risks list: Current year expanded several items with additional clarifying language (e.g., "intellectual property protection difficulties" became "intellectual property protection difficulties or intellectual property theft"; "the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act" became "the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act, and similar local laws").
10. Key personnel: Current year reordered risk factors, moving "key personnel" risk before "new products" risk. Prior year had the opposite order.
11. USMCA reference: Prior year included full name in parenthetical "(USMCA)"; current year dropped the acronym but retained the full name.
12. International operations restructuring language: Current year modified "may not have the positive effects anticipated" to "may not result in the efficiencies or cost savings anticipated."
