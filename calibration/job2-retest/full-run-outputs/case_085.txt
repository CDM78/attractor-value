JUSTIFICATION: The most notable change is the strategic reorientation from a mobile-centric focus to IoT-first positioning, which represents a meaningful business evolution. The removal of the COVID-19 pandemic as a standalone risk factor suggests diminishing pandemic concern, a modestly positive signal. However, the addition of export control/sanctions risks, trade tariff risks, and the explicit acknowledgment of semiconductor supply constraints that "materially limited" revenue growth represent new operational challenges. The entry into long-term capacity agreements creates inventory write-down risk if demand declines. On balance, the changes are roughly offsetting: the strategic repositioning and pandemic normalization are positive, while supply chain constraints, trade policy risks, and new long-term commitments introduce new vulnerabilities. The customer concentration improved slightly (from one 30% customer to a more diversified 22%/17%/11% split).
