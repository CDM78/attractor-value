OBSERVATIONS_C: CHANGED SEVERITY
1. COVID-19 risk factor: Significantly condensed. Prior year included extensive detail about specific rent collection percentages by quarter (88.0%, 97.3%, 97.8%), specific dollar amounts of deferred rent ($4.6 million), numbers of tenants receiving rent relief (270 tenants, 120 receiving deferred rent), and specific dates of rent relief agreements. Current year replaces this with a much shorter summary: "During 2020, a limited number of our tenants took advantage of the relief provided by the local government mandates...we provided rent relief to tenants in the form of deferred rent of approximately $4.6 million...We did not enter into any rent relief agreements granting additional deferrals of base rent during 2021. As of December 31, 2021, we have collected approximately $4.3 million, or 98.0%, of the deferred payments due." The shift from granular quarterly reporting to a concise summary, and the fact that no new deferrals occurred in 2021, suggests the COVID rent risk has substantially diminished.

2. COVID-19 eviction moratorium: Prior year stated most restrictions "are set to expire by March 31, 2021" with uncertainty about extensions. Current year confirms "During 2021, many municipalities allowed their local orders to expire or modified the orders to exclude some tenants" and "Most recently in Los Angeles County...the county's eviction restrictions and rent deferment rights expired on January 31, 2022," leaving only a small number of remaining municipalities.

3. Industry concentration: Updated percentages — Wholesale Trade from 25.4% to 22.6%, Transportation and Warehousing from 24.7% to 21.9%, Light Manufacturing from 18.9% to 20.1%. Modest shifts in tenant mix.

4. Vacancy rates improved: Prior year stated "4.4% of the rentable square footage of our portfolio was vacant" with 15.3% and 14.5% expiring in 2021 and 2022. Current year states "3.7% of the rentable square footage of our portfolio was vacant or under repositioning/redevelopment" with 13.1% and 13.8% expiring in 2022 and 2023.

5. Acquisitions: Prior year stated "approximately 71% of the acquisitions by property count completed by us since our initial public offering ("IPO") were acquired in off-market or lightly marketed transactions." Current year updates to "approximately 75%."

6. Acquisition strategy: Current year adds new language about acquiring non-industrial properties: "We may acquire properties utilized for non-industrial uses, including office properties, where our long-term strategy is to develop, redevelop or reposition the asset into industrial property. Prior to executing our strategy, we may lack non-industrial property management expertise necessary to optimally manage the non-industrial properties."

7. Development risks: Current year adds new bullet point: "non-industrial properties targeted for development, redevelopment or repositioning may be more difficult to manage compared to our industrial properties where we have the most property management expertise."

8. Climate change risk: Current year adds "flooding" to the list of climate-related physical risks and replaces "increased operating costs" with "increased capital expenditures in order to comply with such regulations, as well as increased operating costs."

9. Insurance: Prior year referred to earthquake policies with "material deductibles and self-insured retention." Current year removes "and self-insured retention," mentioning only "material deductibles." Also, current year adds "or increase deductibles" as an option for discontinuing coverage.

10. Cybersecurity: Current year changes "on an ongoing basis we expect to implement annual penetration testing" to "on an ongoing basis we conduct annual penetration testing" — indicating this has moved from planned to implemented. Also adds "although there is no guaranty that the insurance limits and coverage will be sufficient to cover any loss."

UNCERTAINTY_ASSESSMENT: CAN_ASSESS
The changes are clear and substantive. The COVID-19 risk has materially diminished (no new rent deferrals in 2021, 98% of deferred rent collected, eviction moratoriums largely expired). Two new risk factors (inflation and interest rates) reflect the changed macroeconomic environment. The company is expanding into non-industrial property acquisitions, representing a new strategic direction with associated new risks.

SCORE: 3.5

JUSTIFICATION: The risk profile shows a slight net improvement. The most significant positive change is the substantial de-escalation of COVID-19 risk — no new rent deferrals in 2021, 98% recovery of deferred rent, and most eviction moratoriums expired. Vacancy improved from 4.4% to 3.7%, and off-market acquisition percentage increased from 71% to 75%. However, two new macroeconomic risk factors (inflation and interest rate increases) were added, and the expansion into non-industrial property management introduces new operational complexity. On balance, the resolution of COVID-related rent risk modestly outweighs the newly articulated macro risks.
