OBSERVATIONS_A: ADDED RISK FACTORS
1. "We have significant international sales and operations and face risks related to health epidemics which could adversely affect our business and results of operations." — New standalone risk factor explicitly addressing health epidemics/pandemics, with specific discussion of the 2019 novel coronavirus outbreak in China, government-mandated closures of Chinese facilities, and potential impacts on global supply chains. This was not present as a standalone risk factor in the prior year (though force majeure events were mentioned in a bullet point).
2. "Our Wood Processing Systems business can be materially impacted by changes to the global timber supply." — New standalone risk factor covering wildfires, pest damage (mountain pine beetle), and their effects on timber supply, mill closures, and reduced capital expenditures. Prior year did not have this as a separate risk factor.
3. Three new items added to the international operations risk bullet list: "economic sanctions, trade embargoes, or other adverse trade regulations"; "changes in zoning laws that may require relocation of our manufacturing operations"; "disruption from fast-spreading health epidemics which can result in widespread interruption of business operations."

OBSERVATIONS_B: REMOVED RISK FACTORS
NONE REMOVED. All prior year risk factors are present in the current year.

OBSERVATIONS_C: CHANGED SEVERITY
1. Capital equipment revenue: Prior year stated "Approximately 41% of our revenue in 2018 was from the sale of capital equipment." Current year states "Approximately 37% of our revenue in 2019 was from the sale of capital equipment" — a decrease in capital equipment dependency. Also removed reference to SMH acquisition expanding capital equipment capacity, and added material handling industries alongside paper and wood processing.

2. USMCA/trade: Prior year discussed USMCA as awaiting ratification by all three countries. Current year notes "The United States and Mexico have ratified the USMCA, but Canada must still ratify" — progress toward ratification.

3. Tariffs: Current year significantly expands the tariff discussion with detailed listing of Lists 1-4 of Chinese products subject to duties, specific effective dates, and rate changes (including the reduction to 7.5% effective February 2020). Prior year's tariff discussion was much more condensed.

4. China/Russia sanctions: Current year adds "In 2019, the United States continued to expand export control restrictions applicable to certain Chinese firms and commenced its assessment of new controls for 'emerging foundational technologies,' escalating U.S.-China tension over technology competition." Prior year only mentioned Russia sanctions. Sales to Russia decreased from $17.7 million (3% of revenue) to $16.8 million (2% of revenue).

5. Chinese government policies: Current year adds language about potential U.S. restrictions on investment in China — "The president of the United States has indicated that he favors restricting investment by U.S. companies in China; if such restrictions were to become law, or if investment was otherwise restricted, our business would be significantly and adversely affected." Prior year discussion of China waste paper ban updated from "may ban all recovered paper imports by 2020" to "may ban all recovered paper imports by the end of 2020."

6. SMH/Mining: Prior year named the subsidiary as "our new subsidiary, SMH" and stated "Approximately 19% and 10% of SMH's 2018 revenues came from its thermal and metallurgical coal-mining customers." Current year drops "new" (reflecting integration) and reorganizes this into a segment-level risk factor: "Our Material Handling Systems business can be materially impacted by cyclical economic conditions affecting the global mining industry" with "Approximately 6% of our revenue in 2019 was from SMH's mining customers."

7. OSB customer concentration: Updated from "the two largest OSB customers accounted for 14% in 2018, 29% in 2017, and 48% in 2016" to "18% in 2019, 14% in 2018, and 29% in 2017."

8. Digital media impact: Percentage decreased from "Approximately 11% of our revenue in 2018" to "Approximately 10% of our revenue in 2019" for newsprint/printing and writing customers.

9. China capital equipment risk: Prior year mentioned "80% or more of the total order" could be at risk from cancellation. Current year changes to "a significant portion of the total order" — more general language.
